Feature set

UAR focuses on understanding fit before execution.

These are parts of a proposed workflow, not claims about features currently running in this website.

Requirements

Describe what an application needs and the environment in which it is expected to operate.

Compatibility

Compare known requirements with the target environment and keep unknowns visible.

Policy review

Consider security, privacy and operational rules separately from technical fit.

Decision record

Explain the reasoning, conditions and remaining questions before an operator chooses an execution path.

Decision labels

Use labels to explain a decision, not to imply a live scan.

The proposed model uses pass, warning and blocker as plain-language categories. They are illustrative labels on this site, not results from an application review.

Status model

Compatibility and safety remain separate questions. The compatibility guide explains the labels and their limits.
